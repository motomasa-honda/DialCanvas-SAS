## Evidence and decision

Adafruit Product ID **858** lists a compact 5V, 5-pin unipolar geared stepper: 1/16.128 reduction, 516 steps/output revolution, 28mm diameter × 20mm body height, 5mm output shaft, approximately 15N·mm holding torque, and approximately 42Ω per winding. Seller guidance says to keep operation under 6rpm at 5V. This is mechanically/speed plausible inside the existing 50×41mm candidate envelope and the desired decorative gear speed range, but there is no OEM identity or winding-resistance tolerance on the listing.

The ideal ohmic estimate is approximately 119mA/winding at 5V. Seven axes with one winding energized each estimate 0.833A; a 20% reserve requires a guaranteed eFuse limit greater than 1.042A. The current 2.21kΩ eFuse's 1.509A is typical-only and its guaranteed minimum is still unknown, so this is not a pass. Two windings per axis estimate 1.667A, exceeding the current typical eFuse value even before margin. The candidate is therefore **shortlisted only for sample qualification under a one-phase-at-a-time test profile**; torque/start reliability on the actual gear train must decide whether that profile is usable. It is not selected for a production BOM.

Earlier manufacturer candidate PKP223U09A2 remains rejected for the current driver/power architecture (6 leads, 0.95A/phase, no gearhead). `motor_feasibility.py` now reports both the rejected manufacturer item and the conditional seller SKU distinctly.
